# BookStop — Modern Aesthetic Bookstore

## North Star: "Aesthetic Literary Haven"
A clean, Pinterest-inspired reading experience that feels premium yet approachable. Warm minimalism with bold orange accents, generous white space, and soft, rounded UI elements.

## Colors
- **Primary:** #FF7A00 (Vibrant Orange) - Used for primary CTAs, active states, and highlights.
- **Secondary Background:** #F7F7F7 (Light Gray) - For section backgrounds and subtle card grouping.
- **Background:** #FFFFFF (Pure White) - Main interface background to maintain a clean look.
- **Text (Primary):** #111111 (Deep Black) - For headings and body text for high readability.
- **Text (Secondary):** #666666 (Gray) - For metadata, descriptions, and labels.
- **Accent:** #FFA94D (Soft Orange) - For secondary highlights and gentle alerts.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Manrope - Bold, modern, and friendly.
- **Body:** Manrope - Clean and highly legible for long descriptions.
- **Scale:** Large, bold headings for clear hierarchy; compact yet readable labels for book metadata.

## UI Elements
- **Roundness:** 16px to 20px (Extra Rounded) for buttons, cards, and input fields.
- **Shadows:** Soft, diffused shadows (4px to 12px blur) to give cards a floating, premium feel.
- **Spacing:** 8px grid system with abundant whitespace to avoid a cluttered look.
- **Navigation:** 5-tab floating bottom bar (Home, Search, Wishlist, Cart, Profile).

## Component Patterns
- **Book Cards:** Large vertical covers, clean title/author typography, small orange star ratings.
- **Floating Search:** Rounded bar with soft shadow and subtle icons.
- **Buttons:** Fully rounded (pill-shaped) with bold orange fills for primary actions.
- **Categories:** Illustrative cards with soft-colored backgrounds.
